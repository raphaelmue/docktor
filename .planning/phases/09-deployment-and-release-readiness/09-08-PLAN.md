<objective>
Put the custom-certificate feature in front of the user: a place to upload and manage certificates, a choice of certificate source when assigning a domain, and a visible warning before a certificate expires.

Everything underneath now works — plan 09-06 made certificates real and plan 09-06's routes accept uploads, and plan 09-07 made the certificate source govern issuance and taught the poller to watch expiry. None of it is reachable from the browser. This plan closes scope item 4 by wiring the client surface and closing the todo.

Purpose: the user's stated reference point for this feature was Nginx Proxy Manager's certificate management — a list you upload into once and then select from. That is a user interface, not an API. And D-13's expiry warning has no value at all until it renders somewhere a user looks.

Output: a certificates card in Settings with a three-file upload form and a list with delete, a certificate-source choice on the domain-assignment form, an expiry warning badge that arrives live, and scope item 4's todo closed.

**Tracer note:** scope item 4's tracer was plan 09-06's single upload path through every server layer. This plan is its client-side expansion onto an already-proven API, so it carries no tracer task of its own.
